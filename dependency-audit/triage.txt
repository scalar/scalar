LIMITATIONS

packages do not inherit root pnpm patches/overrides. An upstream fix or a
maintained replacement is needed before promising that downstream remediation.

regenerate it. A clean dependency audit does not prove vendor bundles contain no
embedded legacy code. Script execution retains its existing sandbox boundary.
