## How it works

<scalar-steps>
  <scalar-step title="Describe both halves">

Keep endpoints in an OpenAPI document and channels in an AsyncAPI document. [Registry](/products/registry) versions both, and `scalar document lint` checks AsyncAPI with Spectral's AsyncAPI ruleset.

  </scalar-step>
  <scalar-step title="Publish one site">

Add the event API to your Docs project with an [`asyncapi` navigation entry](/products/docs/configuration/navigation#asyncapi), next to your OpenAPI reference. Each channel lists its operations, messages and payload examples.

  </scalar-step>
  <scalar-step title="Generate clients that keep up">

The [SDK generator](/products/sdk-generator) builds clients from your OpenAPI document and, experimentally, [WebSocket and streaming methods from AsyncAPI](/products/sdk-generator/asyncapi), with WebSocket runtimes in TypeScript, Python, PHP, Rust and the CLI. A new matching version of the document rebuilds every target and opens pull requests.

  </scalar-step>
</scalar-steps>

## Events and webhooks

- When one document mixes servers, such as `wss` beside `mqtt`, readers can [filter by protocol and server](/products/api-references/asyncapi#filtering-by-protocol-and-server).
- Self-contained [Avro or Protobuf](/products/api-references/asyncapi#what-renders) payloads render as native fields and types.
- [Webhook helpers](/products/sdk-generator/webhooks) check HMAC-SHA256 signatures, with secret rotation and a timestamp tolerance.
- Generated SDKs [stream server-sent events and newline-delimited JSON](/products/sdk-generator).

## Questions

<scalar-detail title="Do you support MQTT and Kafka?">

In the docs, yes: channels on MQTT, Kafka and other brokers render with their protocol labels and bindings. The SDK generator and the mock server's built-in transports cover WebSocket and HTTP, and the generator reports any broker channel it skips.

</scalar-detail>
<scalar-detail title="Can we keep partner docs private?">

Yes. A [private site](/products/docs/configuration/private-docs) admits your team and the access groups you add, each listing email domains or addresses. Access groups are on Pro and above, SSO/SAML for your own team is on Business, and Scalar has a [SOC 2 Type 1 report](/security).

</scalar-detail>
<scalar-detail title="Our AsyncAPI documents are on 2.x. Do we need to upgrade?">

No. The reference and the SDK generator accept AsyncAPI 1.x and 2.x documents and upgrade them to 3.x on load, with nothing to configure.

</scalar-detail>
